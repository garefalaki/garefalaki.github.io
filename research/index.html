Current research

Evolutionary epigenetics & computational genomics

2023–Present · Wolf Lab, Division of Evolutionary Biology, LMU Munich

My PhD focuses on epigenetic variation in natural populations, particularly DNA methylation repeatability, quantitative genetics and the challenge of separating biological signal from technical variation. I work with RRBS and EM-seq data from wild bird populations and use large-scale statistical and computational genomics approaches to evaluate measurement precision, biological variation and the evolutionary relevance of methylation patterns.

DNA methylation repeatability and technical variation

LMU Munich · Wolf Lab

Developing statistical approaches to quantify DNA methylation repeatability and distinguish biological variation from technical measurement variation in large-scale epigenomic datasets.

Quantitative genetic modelling of epigenetic variation

LMU Munich · Wolf Lab

Using pedigrees of wild blue tits and large EM-seq and RRBS datasets to investigate whether DNA methylation can be modelled as a quantitative trait and to estimate its heritable component.
