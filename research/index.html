Current research

Evolutionary epigenetics & computational genomics

2023–Present · Division of Evolutionary Biology, LMU Munich · Advisor: Prof. Jochen B. W. Wolf

My PhD focuses on biological and technical variation in large-scale DNA methylation datasets from natural bird populations. I develop and benchmark computational and statistical approaches for RRBS and EM-seq data, including replicate concordance, quality control, missingness, batch effects, genome-wide feature integration and measurement reliability at individual CpG sites.

Quantitative genetic modelling of epigenetic variation

2026–Present · LMU Munich

I integrate epigenomic, genomic, pedigree and phenotypic information using mixed models and quantitative-genetic approaches to estimate heritable components of 5mC variation and identify reproducible inter-individual methylation differences.

NGS experimental design & sequencing optimisation

2023–2025 · LMU Munich

I contributed to experimental and computational planning for a large EM-seq project comprising 1,288 sequencing libraries, including read-depth and coverage modelling, technical replication, quality-control thresholds and coverage–cost trade-offs.

Previous research

Computational gene regulation & deep-learning benchmarking

2022 · DIANA Lab, University of Thessaly

Developed reproducible workflows to benchmark microT-CNN and other microRNA target-prediction approaches, analysing canonical and non-canonical miRNA–mRNA interactions and algorithmic performance across large computational datasets.

Human multi-omics & vaccine response

2021–2022 · Biomedical Sciences Research Center “Alexander Fleming”

Analysed transcriptomic, proteomic and clinical data from human blood to characterise molecular responses to BNT162b2 vaccination across clinical cohorts using differential expression, multivariate analysis, correlation analysis and pathway-level interpretation.

Population genomics & transposable elements

2018–2019 · FORTH, Bioinformatics and Population Genomics Group

Developed an open-source workflow to integrate transposable-element annotation, genome-wide SNP variation and population-genomic data in Oryza sativa, investigating selective signals around polymorphic transposable-element insertions.

Plant molecular biology

2018 · FORTH

Worked on molecular validation of CRISPR/Cas9-induced lines and functional characterisation of putative Xylella virulence factors using molecular cloning, bacterial transformation and transient-expression assays.

Chromosomal diversity in Mus musculus domesticus

2016–2017 · University of Patras

Analysed Robertsonian chromosomal variation and hybrid zones using chromosome preparation, karyotyping and cytogenetic approaches.
