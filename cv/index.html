Research Experience

2023–Present

Computational epigenomics & method development

Division of Evolutionary Biology, LMU Munich · Advisor: Prof. Jochen B. W. Wolf. Development and benchmarking of computational approaches for large-scale DNA methylation datasets generated with RRBS and EM-seq, including technical-repeatability analysis, mixed models, variance partitioning, quality control, genomic annotation and HPC-based workflows.

2026–Present

Quantitative genetic modelling of epigenetic variation

LMU Munich. Integration of epigenomic, genomic, pedigree and phenotypic data to estimate heritable components of 5mC variation and distinguish different sources of molecular variation.

2023–2025

NGS experimental design & sequencing optimisation

LMU Munich. Experimental and computational planning for a large EM-seq project comprising 1,288 sequencing libraries, including coverage modelling, technical replication, batch effects, missing data and sequencing depth.

2022

Computational gene regulation & deep-learning benchmarking

DIANA Lab, University of Thessaly. Reproducible benchmarking workflows for microT-CNN and other microRNA target-prediction approaches, including canonical and non-canonical miRNA–mRNA interactions.

2021–2022

Human multi-omics & vaccine response

Biomedical Sciences Research Center “Alexander Fleming”. Transcriptomic, proteomic and clinical-data analysis of BNT162b2 vaccination responses across human cohorts.

2018–2019

Population genomics & transposable elements

FORTH, Bioinformatics and Population Genomics Group. Development of an open-source pipeline integrating transposable-element annotation, SNP variation and population-genomic data to investigate selective signals around polymorphic TE insertions in Oryza sativa.

2018

Plant molecular biology

FORTH. CRISPR/Cas9 molecular validation and functional characterisation of putative Xylella virulence factors using molecular cloning, bacterial transformation and transient-expression assays.

2016–2017

Chromosomal diversity in Mus musculus domesticus

University of Patras. Robertsonian chromosomal variation and hybrid zones studied using chromosome preparation, karyotyping and cytogenetic approaches.
