Astronomy Outreach & Stargazing

2014–2017 · Public astronomy activities · Greece

My astronomy activities during this period extended beyond a single organisation. I participated in public observing sessions, educational activities and stargazing events, including outreach with the Astronomy Society of Patras “Orion”. I also joined independent observing activities, such as a Perseids meteor-shower observation at Kaki Thalassa, overlooking Evia, in August 2016.
