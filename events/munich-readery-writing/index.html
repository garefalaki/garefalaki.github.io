Creative Writing at The Munich Readery

2026 · Writing workshops and community writing · Munich, Germany

Alongside science writing, I have been developing my creative writing practice through workshops and community writing sessions at The Munich Readery. These sessions focus on writing by hand, responding to prompts, experimenting with voice and place, and sharing work in a small group setting when participants choose to do so.

I have taken part in several workshops and writing events in 2026, including:

  • Unlock Your Writing, April 12
  • Munich Write Night, June 11, organised with Deartime
  • Place as Character, July 5, led by Esmé Kaplan Kinsey
  • Unlock Your Writing, September 13
  • The Joys of Journaling, October 4

I also continue to meet informally with some participants for independent writing sessions, keeping the practice social and regular outside the structured workshops.
